## The three UI/UX questions, answered

The report flagged the quiz, the vault suggestions and the weekly digest as
needing a deeper pass. Each RFC opens with that pass rather than deferring it;
the answers, in one place:

- **Quiz → a `quiz` tab kind** (0089 §1), and — the part that makes it this
  app's quiz — **questions sourced from your own marks and questions** (0089 §2):
  two from what you highlighted, two from passages you asked about, one from
  territory you skipped. Sessions are stored with the *source* of each question
  (0089 §4), which is what lets the digest report retention rather than activity.
- **Vault suggestions → a section in the vault inspector** (0091 §1), not a
  Discover tab. Discover is question-shaped and transient; a suggestion has no
  question, belongs to a vault, and must persist until you accept or dismiss it.
  Candidates come from **three fused generators** (0091 §2) — k-hop citation
  traversal, arrival-frequency (co-citation) evidence, and semantic search over
  the vault centroid — combined with the RRF the search service already has.
  The traversal primitive is already written and unused
  (`openalex_lineage_filter`, `#[allow(dead_code)]`).
- **Weekly digest → a `digest` tab under Study mode** (0092 §1). Its subject is
  you over a week, a scope nothing in the shell addresses. **What Study mode
  itself is** — its membership test, what else belongs there, what happens when
  it is empty — turned out to be its own question and got its own RFC (0093).

## Deferred deliberately

Recorded so the intent survives without the app pretending otherwise:

- **`j` / `k` / `o`** stay unbound and their legends are removed (0086 R2). List
  navigation needs a focus model for `PaperList` first.
- **GRAPH and ASK** are removed from the activity rail (0086 R3). A graph is a
  view of the collection and belongs in VAULT; asking already happens from the
  reader and, per 0087, from the vault. STUDY is kept because 0093 gives it a
  destination.
- **Spaced repetition** is out of 0089 — the quiz schema is shaped so a
  scheduler could later read it — and named in 0093 as Study mode's natural next
  inhabitant.
- **Backlinks** ("which notes reference this paper") are out of 0090; forward
  references are extracted at render with no index to keep in sync.
