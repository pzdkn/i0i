## The three UI/UX questions, answered

The report flagged the quiz, the vault suggestions and the weekly digest as
needing a deeper pass. Each RFC opens with that pass rather than deferring it;
the answers, in one place:

- **Quiz → a `quiz` tab kind** (0089 §1). A quiz is a session with a start, a
  sequence and a score, and it needs the width. It sits beside the paper's own
  tab so the question and the text are both open at once — which only works
  because RFC 0084 stopped tabs evicting each other.
- **Vault suggestions → a section in the vault inspector** (0091 §1), not a
  Discover tab. Discover is question-shaped and transient; a suggestion has no
  question, belongs to a vault, and must persist until you accept or dismiss it.
- **Weekly digest → a `digest` tab under a new Study mode** (0092 §1). Its
  subject is you over a week, which is a scope nothing in the shell addresses.
  Study is also where the quiz history belongs, which is why 0089 and 0092 share
  a directory.

## Deferred deliberately

Recorded so the intent survives without the app pretending otherwise:

- **`j` / `k` / `o`** stay unbound and their legends are removed (0086 R2). List
  navigation needs a focus model for `PaperList` first.
- **Backlinks** ("which notes reference this paper") are out of 0090; forward
  references are extracted at render with no index to keep in sync.
- **Spaced repetition** is out of 0089; the quiz schema is shaped so a scheduler
  could later read it.

## Open decisions carried by the RFCs

- 0086 A/B — what `j`/`k`/`o` should mean; whether `cmd+k` is a search or a palette.
- 0088 A — whether a clarification step belongs in paper discovery at all.
- 0089 A/B — re-takeable quizzes; quizzing a vault rather than a paper.
- 0090 A — render a reference as its citation key or its short title.
- 0091 A/B — suggestions on the vault home as well; which vaults get the weekly run.
- 0092 A/B — pushed or pulled digest; what else lives in Study mode.
