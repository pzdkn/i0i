# Learning Research Vault Feature Map

This document captures the initial feature map for a modern Zotero-like app for researchers: part reference manager, part knowledge vault, and part learning system.

## Product Direction

The app helps researchers import and manage papers, discover related work, understand research lineages, curate knowledge, read actively, ask questions across their vault, and turn saved material into study workflows.

## Import / Export

### Import from Zotero / BibTeX

Users can bring their existing research library into the app without starting from scratch. The app should support importing `.bib` files and, eventually, syncing from Zotero.

### Export as BibTeX

Researchers can export selected papers, collections, or their whole vault as BibTeX for LaTeX, Overleaf, papers, theses, grant applications, and other academic writing workflows.

## Paper Discovery

### Paper / Resource Discovery

The app helps users find relevant papers, articles, datasets, talks, and other research resources based on their interests, current vault, or a seed paper.

### Filter Papers by Venue / Year

Search and discovery can be filtered by publication year, conference, journal, author, topic, or relevance. This helps users separate foundational work from recent frontier work.

## Research Graphs

### Link Related Papers / Cited Papers

For any paper, the app shows papers it cites, papers that cite it, and semantically related papers. A single paper becomes an entry point into a broader research area.

### See Paper Lineage

Users can trace how an idea evolved over time: which earlier papers influenced it, which later papers extended it, and where the current paper sits in the intellectual history of the field.

### See Author Lineage

The app can show author networks, advisor and student relationships where available, recurring collaborators, labs, and research communities around a topic.

## Knowledge Vault

### Knowledge Vault Curation

Users do not merely store PDFs. They curate a personal vault of concepts, papers, claims, open questions, notes, and links between ideas.

### Organize Vault

Users can manually organize their vault into folders, projects, topics, reading lists, research questions, or paper collections.

### Self-Organizing Vault

The app can automatically suggest clusters, tags, duplicate merges, related concepts, paper groups, and missing connections. The user remains in control, while the system reduces organizational friction.

## Reading / Annotation

### Take Notes Linked to Paper

Notes are attached directly to papers, sections, figures, claims, or paragraphs. This keeps notes traceable back to their sources instead of turning them into detached fragments.

### Ask Questions / Mark Paragraphs

While reading, users can highlight confusing, important, or questionable paragraphs and ask targeted questions about them. The app becomes a reading companion rather than only a storage system.

## Vault Q&A

### Ask Questions to Your Vault

Users can ask questions across their whole research library, such as "What are the main arguments against method X?" or "Which papers in my vault discuss sparse autoencoders for interpretability?" Answers should cite the underlying papers and notes.

## Study Mode

### Study

The vault becomes a learning system. The app can turn papers and notes into review prompts, concept checks, spaced repetition cards, reading plans, or guided explanations.
