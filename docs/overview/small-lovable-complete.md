# IDE for knowledge curation

# Target Audience
- Academics and Researchers
- Industrial Researchers
- Curious Nerds

## Secondary Audience
- Knowledge Workers 
- Growth-minded people

# Key Pain Points:
- Zotero organizes literature
- Obsidian organizes notes
- Claude/ AI lets you discover and chat with papers. But results and insights from chats are transient. Search is based on current chat.
- Pain point: No central place for organizing literature and distilling, persisting and organizting insights from discussions with AI

### Killer Features (SLC)
- Configurable Search Agent:
    - number, sources, venues, date filter, cron job, keywords
- Insights from paper and collections of papers
    - Chat with papers
    - Chat with collections of papers
    - Distill insights into notes and perssistant artifacts.
- Agentic usage:
    - This is like agentic researcher. You can wire together agents. Let them interact. Hand off artifcats. Discuss and elaborate in multiple rounds.
    - This is a novel feature not intended in the original feature map. This is up to discussion.
    - Every week find the five most interessting explainable AI papers relevant to my research, and summarize them with respect to the problem they sovlve, and their methodology
    - Find all papers between 2011 and 2022 that use the MS COCO dataset.
    - Among the papers in the interpretability find a common unadressed gap, that can be formulated into a research question.
    - Interface can be like n8n, simulink.
