# Pitch

**TLDR:** I think the real opportunity in AI is education. As models converge, your edge is no longer the model but how deeply you understand your field, and education is how you get there. I am building a tool for exactly that.

The most exciting thing about these models is how much more a skilled person can get out of them. Give one to a beginner and to Terence Tao: Tao makes progress on hard proofs while the beginner gets a confident wrong answer, because Tao knows what to ask and can tell when the output is nonsense. That gap is education, and education is trainable. So I am building a Knowledge Curation IDE, an environment for learning a field fast the way a code IDE is for writing software. We start with researchers, who drown in papers with no time to absorb them. i0i holds their papers and surfaces new ones, but the real bet is what it builds underneath: a learning profile that models what you already understand and what you do not, drawn quietly from the notes you take, the questions you park, and the answers you save. Because it knows where your understanding ends, it can put the right thing in front of you next, material at the edge of your ability, hard enough to stretch you but not so hard you bounce off. That is the part nobody has built, a tool that knows what you know, knows what you do not, and keeps handing you the next thing you are ready to learn.
